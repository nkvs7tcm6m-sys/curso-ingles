Los conectores en inglés: because, so, although, however

Los conectores unen frases y hacen que tu inglés suene fluido. Con ellos pasas de frases sueltas a explicar razones, contrastes y secuencias.

Causa y resultado

because da la razón (I stayed home because it was raining). so da el resultado (It was raining, so I stayed home). because of va con un sustantivo: because of the rain.

Contraste

but, although / even though + frase completa, despite / in spite of + sustantivo o -ing, y however entre dos frases: I wanted it. However, it was too expensive.

Secuencia y finalidad

First, then, after that, finally para ordenar. to, in order to, so that para decir para qué: I study English to find a better job.

Ejemplos con audio

Me quedé en casa porque llovía.
I stayed at home because it was raining.
Llovía, así que cogí un paraguas.
It was raining, so I took an umbrella.
Aunque estaba cansado, terminé.
Although I was tired, I finished.
A pesar del tráfico, llegamos a tiempo.
Despite the traffic, we arrived on time.
El hotel era barato. Sin embargo, era ruidoso.
The hotel was cheap. However, it was noisy.
Estudio inglés para viajar.
I study English in order to travel.

Errores comunes

  • Although it was cold, but we went out. → Although it was cold, we went out.
    No se usa but con although.
  • Despite of the rain... → Despite the rain...
    Despite no lleva of.
  • because of it was raining → because it was raining
    Because of va con sustantivo.

Practica ahora

Preguntas frecuentes

¿Cuál es la diferencia entre because y so?

Because introduce la razón; so, el resultado.

¿Cómo uso however?

Entre dos frases, con coma después: It was cheap. However, it was bad.
