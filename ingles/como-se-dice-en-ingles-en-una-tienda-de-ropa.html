Cómo se dice en inglés en una tienda de ropa

Comprar ropa en inglés implica hablar de tallas, colores, precios y probadores. Con estas frases podrás preguntar, probarte algo y pagar con confianza.

Preguntar y pedir ayuda

Can I help you? — I'm just looking, thanks. Do you have this in a bigger size? Do you have it in blue? Where are the fitting rooms? Can I try this on, please?

Tallas y ajuste

What size are you? I'm a medium. It's too tight / too loose / too long / too short. It doesn't fit me. It suits you! Do you have a smaller one?

Precios y pago

How much is this? Is it on sale? Can I pay by card? Can I get a refund? Here is your receipt. In the UK trousers; in the US pants.

Errores comunes

  • I want to probe this shirt. → I want to try on this shirt.
    Probar ropa es try on, no probe.
  • It doesn't fit to me. → It doesn't fit me.
    Fit no lleva to.
  • How many costs this? → How much is this?
    Para precios se usa how much.

Preguntas frecuentes

¿Cómo pido una talla más pequeña?

Do you have this in a smaller size?

¿Qué significa on sale?

En rebajas o con descuento: This shirt is on sale.
