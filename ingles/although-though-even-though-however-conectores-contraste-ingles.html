Although, though, even though y however: conectores de contraste en inglés

Para decir aunque, pero o sin embargo el inglés tiene varias opciones. Se parecen mucho en significado, pero cambian la estructura de la frase.

Although, though y even though

Van seguidos de sujeto y verbo: Although it was cold, we went out. Even though es más enfático: Even though she was tired, she kept working. Though es informal y puede ir al final: It was cold. We went out, though.

But y however

But une dos ideas dentro de la misma frase: I like tea, but I prefer coffee. However abre una frase nueva: I like tea. However, I prefer coffee. Va seguido de coma.

Despite e in spite of

Van seguidos de un sustantivo o un -ing, no de una oración completa: Despite the rain, we played. In spite of being tired, he went out.

Ejemplos con audio

Aunque llovía, salimos a pasear.
Although it was raining, we went for a walk.
Me gusta el café, pero prefiero el té.
I like coffee, but I prefer tea.
Aunque es rico, no es feliz.
Even though he is rich, he is not happy.
La película era larga. Sin embargo, fue genial.
The film was long. However, it was great.
A pesar del tráfico, llegamos a tiempo.
Despite the traffic, we arrived on time.
Era caro. Aun así, lo compré.
It was expensive. I bought it, though.

Errores comunes

  • Although it was cold, but we went out. → Although it was cold, we went out.
    Con although no se usa but.
  • Despite it was late, we stayed. → Despite the time, we stayed. / Although it was late, we stayed.
    Despite va con sustantivo, no con una oración.
  • However, I like tea, however I prefer coffee. → I like tea; however, I prefer coffee.
    However no une dos frases con coma: usa punto y coma o punto.

Preguntas frecuentes

¿Though y although son lo mismo?

Casi. Though es más informal y puede ir al final de la frase.

¿Despite e in spite of son iguales?

Sí, significan lo mismo: a pesar de.
